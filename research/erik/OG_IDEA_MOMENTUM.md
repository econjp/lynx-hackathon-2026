## Accounting specific to this study

Close series only; desired position at t executes next close. Gross P&L uses X.shift(2) times daily price changes; fees use 0.02 times absolute X.diff().shift(2), with no price or volatility multiplier. Annualization is 252. These conventions intentionally differ from the existing example-strategy README and evaluator. Existing strategy files are not replaced. Organizer scorer was absent from the original local inputs; the notebook discloses sample standard deviation and bias-corrected skewness/excess kurtosis.
